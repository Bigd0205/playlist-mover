How to get the two Client IDs

Spotify (needs Spotify Premium on your account)

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in.
  2. Create app. Any name. Tick Web API.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, paste the address above and add it.
  4. Save, open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID into the box above.
  5. Under User Management, add the email of anyone else who will use it (up to 5 people). You don't need to add yourself.

Tidal

  1. Go to developer.tidal.com/dashboard and log in with your Tidal account.
  2. Create an app. Any name.
  3. In the app's settings, add the redirect URI above.
  4. Turn on the scopes playlists.read, playlists.write, search.read and user.read.
  5. Copy the Client ID into the box above. You don't need the Client Secret.
